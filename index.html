How We Met

2018 · Hyderabad

The First Meeting

Fate brought them together at a Carnatic kacheri in Tyagaraja Gana Sabha. Kethan couldn't stop sneaking glances at Indu, who was completely captivated by the veena performance.

2020 · Araku Valley

The First Trip Together

Surrounded by misty coffee estates and the scent of tribal silver, their friendship quietly blossomed into something neither could deny any longer.

2019 · Vijayawada

First Date

Over filter coffee and crispy pesarattu at a tiny café near the Krishna river, Kethan finally gathered the courage to tell her what everyone else already knew.

2024 · Tirumala

The Proposal

After Lord Venkateswara's darshan, with the temple bells ringing in the distance, Kethan presented a ring — and Indu said yes before he could even finish the question.
